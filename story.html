The Beginning

Where It All Started

Urban Bites began in 2024 as a single obsession: what if fast food could taste like fine dining? Not in a gimmicky way. Not with white tablecloths. But real — with fire, craft, and relentless attention to every ingredient.

Our founders grew up eating on Karachi's streets, then trained in professional kitchens across the city. They saw a gap — food that moves fast but never cuts corners — and decided to fill it.
